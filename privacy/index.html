Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

About this application

Local AI Publisher is a private desktop application used by its owner to create, review, and publish videos to accounts authorized by that owner.

Information processed

The application may process OAuth authorization tokens, video files, titles, descriptions, tags, and publishing status information.

Storage

Credentials and publishing information are stored locally on the owner's Windows computer. They are not published to this website.

Sharing

Video content and metadata are transmitted only to platforms explicitly selected and authorized by the user, including TikTok and YouTube.

Deletion and revocation

The user can disconnect their accounts from the application and revoke access through the security settings of TikTok or Google.
